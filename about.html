Our Mission & Philosophy

We Believe Every Child Has an Engineer Waiting to Be Unlocked

At RoboBricks, we don't believe in passive screen time or following dry instruction manuals. We treat every child as a creator. Through tactile LEGO® gears, microcontrollers, and collaborative problem-solving, kids learn to hypothesize, build, test, fail safely, and rebuild with joy.

Our Journey

How RoboBricks Came to Life

From a small neighborhood basement club to nationwide recognized STEM maker labs.

2018

The Garage Lab

Founded by two robotics engineer parents with 6 LEGO Mindstorms kits and 8 curious neighborhood kids in Austin, TX.

2021

First Dedicated Hub

Opened our first official learning center equipped with Spike Prime kits, 3D printers, and custom arena tables.

2023

FLL National Honors

Our student team 'RoboDragons' captured 1st place Robot Performance at the FIRST LEGO League Regional Championship.

Today

14 Locations & 1,450+ Kids

Now offering after-school, weekend, and summer camps across 3 states with dedicated parent portals and mobile apps.
